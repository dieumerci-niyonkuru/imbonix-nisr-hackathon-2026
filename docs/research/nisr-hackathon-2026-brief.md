## Tracks
1. **Agricultural Productivity**: irrigation, soil conservation, post-harvest storage, value addition, exports.
2. **Financial Inclusion & Poverty Reduction**: financial exclusion, poverty dynamics, impact of social protection programmes.
3. **Open Innovation**: any sector, aligned to NST2 / Vision 2050.

## Eligibility and rules
- Undergraduate or master's students (any field) with a valid student ID.
- Teams of **2 students**, at least one a Rwandan citizen. Members may come from different universities.
- Past 1st–3rd place NISR winners cannot enter.
- **NISR data is mandatory**. Open or public external data may be added.
- AI tools are allowed but their use must be **disclosed**. You must declare the work is original and cite sources.
- **All IP transfers to NISR** when you enter.
- Deliverables: **public GitHub repo, deployed app link, and documentation**. Any language is allowed.

## Scoring criteria
Problem understanding and NST2/V2050 alignment · Data use and methodology · Tech innovation · Usability and design · Tangible impact.
Compared with 2024/2025 (Data 25, Impact 25, UI/UX 15, Creativity 15, Relevance 20), UI/UX and innovation now carry more weight.

## Prizes
Laptops for the top 3 teams. Final-year Rwandan winners may be offered NISR internships.

## Past editions
- 2024: youth (ages 0–25) problems. Required ≥1 NISR dataset plus ≥1 external dataset and a Python/R dashboard.
- 2025: tracks were exports, hidden hunger, climate/disaster, GIS Season C, and open innovation. The top 15 went to a 7-day bootcamp in Musanze.
- Past winners built interactive dashboards on GDP, CPI, LFS, and SAS data. Winner projects were not published.

## Headline numbers for each track
- **EICV7 (2023/24)**: poverty is 27.4% (39.8% in 2017), extreme poverty 5.4%. 14 of 30 districts are above the national average. District decks include sector-level poverty figures.
- **FinScope 2024**: 96% of adults are financially included, 92% formally. 4% (~316k) are excluded. Mobile money is the main driver.
- **SAS**: improved-seed use was 20.9% in 2026B and 18% in 2025B. Irrigation was 14.4% in 2026A. Small-scale farmers use improved seed at 16.7% vs 72.1% for large-scale farmers.
- **Other recent releases**: Q2 2026 GDP +9.4% y/y. Aug 2026 CPI 15.7% y/y. LFS 2026, DHS 2025, Census 2022.
