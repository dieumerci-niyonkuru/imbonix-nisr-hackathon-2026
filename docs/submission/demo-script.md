## 90-second pitch

> Rwanda solved **access** to finance — 96% are included. But only 10% are financially healthy, and the households left
> behind cluster where poverty, poor nutrition and shocks overlap. IMBONIX brings NISR's published figures together for
> all 30 districts and 416 sectors, labels every value for trust, and turns them into clear starting points for action —
> paired with an on-device AI assistant that answers in plain language. It is open source, independent, and always
> demonstrable with no API key.

## 3-minute walkthrough (mapped to the five criteria)

### 1. The problem (Criterion 1 — Problem understanding & relevance) — 30 seconds

1. Open the **homepage** — the hero shows the four headline figures
2. Scroll to the **gap chart**: 96% included, only 10% healthy — the 86-point gap
3. Point out **"Aligned to national priorities"** — Vision 2050, NST2, the Financial Inclusion Roadmap

### 2. The data (Criterion 2 — Data use & methodology) — 30 seconds

1. Open **Data & methodology** (`/data`) — show the sources table with trust labels
2. Click any chart — point out the **source line, year, and trust label** beneath it
3. Show the **CSV download** button on a homepage chart — every figure is downloadable with its source

### 3. The tech (Criterion 3 — Tech innovation) — 40 seconds

1. Open **IMBONIX AI** (the floating badge) — type "how poor is Huye?"
   - Show the **grounded answer** with source, rank and links
   - Point out: on-device, no API key, no network call, works offline
2. Open the **district map** (`/poverty-dynamics/district-map`) — select a district, show sectors
3. Open the **intervention planner** — choose "Poverty", "Rural households", "Huye" — show the evidence and options

### 4. The design (Criterion 4 — Usability & design) — 20 seconds

1. Show the **mobile view** (resize the browser or use device toolbar)
2. Show a chart's **table view** toggle — accessibility for screen readers
3. Show the **chart library** (`/data/chart-library`) — all charts in one place

### 5. The impact (Criterion 5 — Tangible impact) — 30 seconds

1. Open **priority districts** (`/social-protection/priority-districts`) — show the 7 policy levers
2. Show how each lever **flags the 10 most affected districts** with one published figure and a stated rule
3. Close with: "A district planner opens IMBONIX, sees their biggest need, and knows what programme to push — all from one trustworthy, sourced platform."

## Who uses IMBONIX

| User type | What they do | Key page |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **District planners** (primary user) | See their district's biggest needs, decide where to allocate resources | District profiles, Intervention planner |
| **Policymakers** (MINALOC, MINECOFIN, LODA) | Use 7 policy levers to flag priority districts | Priority districts |
| **Rwandan citizens** | Look up their own district in plain language | Districts, IMBONIX AI |
| **Development partners** (WFP, UNICEF, AFR) | See where vulnerabilities overlap to target programmes | Overlapping needs |
| **Researchers** | Access every figure with source, year and trust label; use the JSON API | Data & methodology |
| **Civil society** | Follow programmes with open, sourced figures | District profiles |
