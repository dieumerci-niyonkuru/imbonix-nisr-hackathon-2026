# AI assistance disclosure

> **Submission package:** [Index](README.md) · [Solution overview](solution-overview.md) · [Demo script](demo-script.md) · [IMBONIX AI](../ai-assistant.md) · **AI disclosure**

AI coding assistants were used on this project for:

- drafting and reviewing code for the web app, the API, the data scripts and the tests;
- drafting documentation;
- desk research support, such as indexing the NISR microdata catalog and cross-checking transcribed figures against
  their published tables.

Every statistic shown by IMBONIX comes from a published NISR or partner source cited beside it; no figure was generated by
an AI tool. The team reviewed the work and is responsible for every figure, claim and line of code in the submission.

Before submitting, the team should record here the specific tools used and any other details the NISR competition rules
require.
