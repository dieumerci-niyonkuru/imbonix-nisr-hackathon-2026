# Data Source Plan

## Primary sources

- NISR poverty and social-protection publications
- Integrated Household Living Conditions Survey (EICV)
- Labour Force Survey
- 2022 Population and Housing Census district profiles
- NISR Central Data Catalog and aggregate portals

## Verified dashboard slice

The national financial-inclusion cards use the NISR FinScope Survey 2024 publication.

- Dataset: Rwanda FinScope Survey 2024
- Fieldwork: 6 September–8 October 2024
- Sample: 14,000 households
- Geography shown: Rwanda, national estimates
- Source page: https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/business-establishment-finance-trade/finscope-survey-2024
- Report: https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2024-09/Rwanda-Finscope-2024-Report_compressed.pdf
- Access and reuse: the report is public; verify microdata access and study-specific terms in NISR NADA before reuse.

The district profiles and signal cards currently shown in the prototype are illustrative demo content, not observed NISR district estimates or measured intervention impacts.

## Secondary sources

- Public geospatial and service-location data
- OpenStreetMap where appropriate
- Public population-density or connectivity indicators

## Validation gate

Before modelling, record the dataset title, year, access status, sample design, weights, geography, variable definitions, missingness, licence and permitted redistribution status.
